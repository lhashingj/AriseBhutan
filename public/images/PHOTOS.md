# Arise Bhutan — Image Filenames

Place your photos here with these exact filenames.
Next.js will auto-convert them to WebP/AVIF for fast loading.

## Required filenames

### Logo
- `logo.jpeg` — Arise Bhutan logo (black & white Buddha head)

### Tiger's Nest / Monasteries
- `tigers-nest-1.jpg` — Main iconic Tiger's Nest view (used in hero slide 1)
- `tigers-nest-2.jpg` — Tiger's Nest with prayer flags (used in Why Bhutan grid)
- `tigers-nest-3.jpg` — Tiger's Nest close-up / different angle
- `monastery-architecture.jpg` — Temple or dzong architecture
- `punakha-dzong.jpg` — Punakha Dzong (or any ornate dzong)

### Cham Dance / Festivals
- `cham-dance-blue.jpg` — Blue costume Cham dancer (used in hero slide 2)
- `cham-dance-red.jpg` — Red costume Cham dancer (used in Why Bhutan grid)
- `cham-dance-orange.jpg` — Orange robes Cham dancer
- `cham-dance-masks.jpg` — Multiple Cham dancers with masks
- `cham-dance-crowd.jpg` — Festival crowd scene
- `festival-scene.jpg` — Any other festival photo

### Sunrise / Landscapes
- `sunrise-summit.jpg` — Person at sunrise with arms outstretched (hero slide 3)
- `sunrise-silhouette.jpg` — Silhouette/sunrise scene (used in CTA banner)
- `prayer-flags-mountains.jpg` — Prayer flags with mountain peaks (hero slide 4)
- `prayer-flags-valley.jpg` — Prayer flags in valley (used in Why Bhutan grid)
- `mountain-valley.jpg` — Mountain valley landscape

### Food
- `ema-datshi.jpg` — National dish (used in Why Bhutan grid)
- `red-rice-dish.jpg` — Rice dish
- `chilli-fritters.jpg` — Chilli fritters
- `bhutanese-food-spread.jpg` — Food spread/table

### Wildlife
- `yellow-billed-magpie.jpg` — Yellow-billed Blue Magpie bird

### People & Guides
- `pilgrimage-group.jpg` — Large pilgrim group
- `guide-tourist.jpg` — Guide with tourist(s)

## Tips for fast loading
- Resize images to max 1920px wide before copying here
- Next.js will handle WebP conversion and responsive sizes automatically
- Use free tools: squoosh.app or tinypng.com to compress first

# Arise Bhutan — Digital Marketing & Ad Campaign Strategy

**Objective:** Attract budget-conscious yet premium international travellers by owning one message the Bhutan market under-serves: **transparent, all-inclusive pricing from $200–$250/day — mandatory $100 SDF included.**

**Primary conversion:** Free quote request at `arisebhutan.com/free-quote` (24-hour personal response, $0 to enquire).
**Secondary conversions:** Adventure Builder submission (`/adventure-builder`), WhatsApp conversation start.

---

## 1. Positioning & Core Message Pillars

Most Bhutan operators hide the SDF and quote vague "from" prices that balloon later. Our wedge:

| Pillar | Proof point | Where it converts |
|---|---|---|
| **Radical price transparency** | $200–$250/day *total*, line-itemised: hotel + all meals + guide + private car + permits + **$100 SDF** | Ads, landing page price card |
| **Premium, fully private** | No group buses, ever. Private guide + vehicle at 3-star prices | Ad copy, About page |
| **Zero-risk enquiry** | Free personalised quote in 24h, no deposit to ask | Every CTA |
| **We handle the bureaucracy** | Visa, permits, SDF processing done for you; live document tracking in the client portal | Honeymoon + group ads |
| **Group-friendly tech** | One booking reference shared by the whole party; every guest sees the itinerary & documents in their own portal | Group ads (unique — competitors have nothing like it) |

**Tone:** warm, confident, concrete numbers. Never "cheap" — always "transparent" and "all-inclusive." The audience is *value-seeking premium*, not backpacker.

### 2.2 On-page checklist (apply to every tour page)

- Title tag pattern: `{Tour Name} — {N} Days from ${price} All-Inclusive | Arise Bhutan`
- One H1, price + SDF mention in the first 100 words, FAQ block at the bottom.
- **Schema markup:** `TouristTrip` + `Offer` (price, currency) + `FAQPage` + `AggregateRating` where reviews exist. Rich results with prices dramatically lift CTR for cost-related queries.
- Internal links: every blog post → relevant tour page → `/free-quote`.
- Image alt text with destination names ("Tiger's Nest Monastery, Paro, Bhutan").

### 2.3 Content calendar (one post/month, cost-cluster first)

1. The Real Cost of a Bhutan Trip in 2026 — full price breakdown (pillar)
2. Bhutan's SDF Explained: What the $100/night Actually Funds
3. Bhutan on $250/Day: A Premium Trip Without the Luxury Markup
4. Paro Tshechu 2026: Dates, What to Expect, How to Book (publish ~5 months before)
5. Bhutan Visa Step-by-Step for {US/UK/AU} Travellers

### 2.4 Technical & authority

- Keep `sitemap.xml` submitted in Search Console; fix the canonical www redirect (already in `next.config.mjs`).
- Pursue listings/backlinks: Bhutan DOT operator directory, travel association pages, guest posts on Himalaya-travel blogs, Reddit r/travel & r/Bhutan (genuinely helpful answers linking the SDF explainer).
- Collect Google Business Profile reviews after every tour — "Arise Bhutan reviews" is a bottom-funnel query we must own.

## 3. Meta (Facebook/Instagram) Ads Framework

### 3.1 Campaign structure

| Stage | Campaign | Audience | Creative | Budget |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| TOF (cold) | Traffic → `/free-quote` (optimise Landing Page Views, then switch to Leads once pixel has ~50 events) | Interest stacks below | Ads A/B/C | 60% |
| MOF (warm) | Engagement retargeting — video viewers 50%+, IG engagers 90 days | Festival reel + price-breakdown carousel | 15% |
| BOF (hot) | Website retargeting — visited `/tours` or `/free-quote` without submitting, 30 days | Testimonial + "Your quote is one message away" | 25% |

### 3.2 Audiences (cold interest stacks)

- **Geo:** US, UK, Australia, Canada, Singapore, Germany (highest Bhutan visitor spend); India separately with INR/SAARC messaging.
- **A — Solo/adventure:** interests: Trekking, Backpacking, Lonely Planet, Nepal travel + age 28–50.
- **B — Honeymoon:** engaged (6 months), newlywed, honeymoon interest + age 25–40.
- **C — Culture/group:** Buddhism, meditation, National Geographic Travel, cultural tourism + age 35–65.
- Lookalike 1–3% of quote-form leads once ≥100 leads collected.

### 3.3 Pixel events

`PageView` → `ViewContent` (tour pages) → **`Lead` (quote form submit — primary optimisation event)** → `Contact` (WhatsApp click).

### 3.4 Creative guidelines

- Lead visual: Tiger's Nest or prayer-flag ridge shots (already in `/public/images`) — native-feel photography outperforms polished graphics for travel.
- Always show the **price with SDF included** in the first 3 seconds / first line — it's the scroll-stopper.
- Format mix: 1 static (price-card), 1 carousel (day-by-day itinerary), 1 Reel (9:16 POV hike) per ad set.

### 3.5 KPI benchmarks (travel, cold)

CTR ≥ 1.2% · CPC ≤ $0.90 · Landing→lead ≥ 8% · Cost per lead ≤ $12 · Lead→booking 3–5% target.
